# Register Atlas — every document culture in the world

One card game, many document cultures ("registers"). Each faction/order prints its own cards in its own medium. This file is the master inventory: what's adopted for Set 01, what's banked for the roadmap. References in `art/reference/register-*.png`; validated generations in `art/output/`.

### The punk sheet (Glitchwrights Black Catalog)

`register-glitchwrights-punk-black-catalog.png` — street-illicit, sticker-bombed, human riders, "ILLICIT EDITION", Misprint King K-404 ("NOT SUPPOSED TO BE HERE"). Tom loves it. Resolution: **merge its grit into the zine register** (the zine's bones + punk's attitude), AND keep "the Black Catalog" as an in-world product layer: bootleg/secret-rare promo cards. A misprint king that "shouldn't exist" is the perfect secret rare. Note: punk sheet uses human characters — if kept, they're riders/handlers, not the pieces themselves.

### The Null Choir (empty forms) — adopted for the Hush

Debossed piece-shaped ABSENCES in paper, dashed outlines, cut-out silhouettes, bitten corners, "SIX PIECES. NO VICTORY." Canon merge: **the Null Choir is the Hush's formal name for themselves** (civilians say "the Hush") — fits the Quiet Queen's "she hums along." Mechanics harvested: Withdraw ("take back a move before it was made"), Absence Step ("move to a square that is not there"), Final Step ("advance one, then remove this"), Empty File. These are the Hush's 16 in embryo.

## ROADMAP BANK (Set 02+ / special products)

- **The Continuity Council** (`register-continuity-council`) — bureaucratic-modernist spec sheets, zoning classes, barcodes. STRONG immediate use: issue Set 01's **2 rules cards + 6 piece-suite cards** as Council documents — "issued under authority of the Continuity Protocol, ver 7.3." The base rules of chess ARE the bureaucracy; every faction's glitches deface it. Also a full lawful faction later.
- **Static CRT ghosts** (`register-static-crt-ghosts`) — phosphor datamosh spectres. Candidate: avatar cards for the board-sides themselves (Signal/Static), or a Set 02 faction. Overlaps Null Choir emotionally; differentiate by tech-haunting vs paper-absence.
- **The Promoted** (`register-the-promoted-stained-glass`) — stained-glass radiance, promotion-as-transcendence cult. Set 02 faction candidate; gorgeous foil target.
- **The Diagonal Orders** (`register-diagonal-orders-persian`) — Persian miniature illumination. Proves the world is global (Ivy City is one city of many). Set 02/03; handle with cultural care + real reference.
- **The Builders machine schematics** (`register-builders-machine-schematics`) — ⚠ CANON GUARDRAIL: lore forbids revealing the builders. Acceptable dodge: these depict *recovered machinery, cataloged* — artifacts only, never makers. Use sparingly (a 2-3 card "recovered artifacts" cameo, maybe the fifth-chair tease).
- **Fermata & Daughters, Guild of the Suspended Moment** (`register-fermata-and-daughters-clockmakers`) — Victorian clockmaker atelier, wax seals. Canon-expanding: Elena's family shop as a guild of held time. Mini-suite / promo candidate; pairs with the Sealed Move.

## Standing principles

1. Every register is a *document culture* — cards are artifacts printed by someone in-world.
2. Factions never share a register; the frame system + universal card back carry product unity.
3. Piece-silhouette rule holds for Awakened characters; humans allowed as piece-suite "memories," Marginalia archivists, and (if kept) Black Catalog riders.
4. The builders stay unrevealed. Machinery yes, makers never.
