## The art architecture — REVISED (Tom's call, July 2026)

**SUPERSEDED:** the "collage as universal binding with mounted specimens" test below worked technically but reads flat — a painting taped into a page is a postcard, not a card. **New architecture: the document principle survives, the shared binding dies.** Each faction produces its own cards in its own documentation culture, edge to edge: Marginalia = archive collage pages (theirs alone); Glitchwrights = riso/photocopy workshop zine patch-notes (fluoro inks, misregistration-as-glitch, hazard tape, stamps); Errantry & Hush = TBD, each fully its own medium. The frame compositor + shared card back keep the product coherent.

### Glitchwrights register: VALIDATED (art/output/zine-*.png)

The Foundry's document culture is the **workshop-zine patch-note**: riso/screenprint on newsprint, fluoro pink + electric teal + acid yellow + warm black, photocopier grit, misregistration intensifying with glitch energy, hazard tape, staples, spiral-notebook tears, grease-pencil corrections ("PUSH HARDER", "MORE TAPE HERE"), TESTED stamps, version numbers, sticker-bombs, diagonal kinetic composition. All four tests landed first try: zine-volt (triple offset print), zine-solder (exploded jetpack diagram), zine-overclock (a CHESS CLOCK with fanned hands — effect cards work in pure imagery), zine-foundry (zine-cover scene: the arch, the daemon-jar wall). Prompt preamble lives in the zine test script; reuse as the Glitchwrights `ZINE` recipe.

Open: Errantry + Hush document cultures. Current candidates — Errantry: painted waymarker panels (weathered wood, carved distances, votive smoke); Hush: letterpress deep-impression on near-blank paper, embossing, iridescent Nullity as the only color.

**Original (superseded) test:** collage binding with faction specimens mounted inside that LEAK. Proven across all four factions in one generation each (see `art/output/collage-volt/colossus/nightclerk/fermata-*.png`):
- Volt's riso misprint scorches the page and lifts its own tape (kinetic joy survives ✓)
- The Colossus's cyan veins run off the plate into the paper; pressed moss taped alongside
- The Night Clerk's lamplight falls on the page itself; a due-date card reads JUL 24
- Fermata: the page's red stitching thread becomes her scarf inside the mount and exits the other side; margins near her are erased blank. Date stamp: JUN 14 — the canon date, unprompted.

The binding prompt preamble lives in the collage-test script; canonical copy:

> Full-bleed trading card artwork in the Marginalia archive-collage register: a scanned page assembled from aged cream and tea-stained papers with torn edges, archival tape, a small circular MARGINALIA paper seal, ticket stubs with stamped catalog numbers, faint typewriter fragments and handwritten tracery annotations in the margins, and one line of red thread stitched through the composition. Matte, flat, scanned-document lighting — an artifact, NOT a glossy digital painting. At the center, mounted like a museum specimen: [FACTION SPECIMEN]. [HOW THE SPECIMEN LEAKS PAST ITS MOUNT]. Composition: one clear focal specimen; lower quarter calmer for rules panel; kid-friendly; incidental archival stamps welcome, NO large title text, NO rules text.

## Mechanics to playtest (from the ChatGPT suites)

- **SEALED MOVE** (from `collage-suite-keepers-of-repair`): "Write a move (from current position) and seal it face down. On a future turn, reveal and make that move." Adjournment-as-mechanic — deeply canon, possibly the game's signature card. Test legality edge cases (what if the move is illegal when revealed? Suggested: it becomes an unbound move — even more on-theme).
- **Location cards** (from `collage-suite-places` and `collage-suite-holes-in-reality`): places as playable cards — The Seam ("move as if along the edge of boards"), The Rain That Loops ("at the start of each turn, repeat the last move made"), The Door That Forgot to Close. Candidate new card type or a re-skin of Daemons/Events as *Holes in Reality*. The "holes" framing ties directly to Story 01's unfinished-game holes.
- **Fermata / Pause** ("end your turn; freeze all pieces you control until you move") — time-hold as the Hush signature.
- Effect suite names worth keeping: Rook Glide, Silent Square, Bishop Echo, Flicker, Recall, Anchor (icon-row layouts on `collage-suite-field-kit-7-effects`).
- Piece-archetype names: Walking Knight, Remembering Pawn, Diagonal Prophet, Living Rook, Continuity King, Memory Queen — candidates for the 6 piece-suite cards' titles.
